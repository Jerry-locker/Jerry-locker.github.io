Xiaolei Lang (Jerry)

I am currently a research engineer at Xiaomi Autonomous Driving under the Future Star Program, working on world simulators with a focus on feed-forward 3D/4D reconstruction and video world models.

I obtained my Ph.D. degree in 2025 from the College of Control Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, advised by Researcher Jiajun Lv, Prof. Xingxing Zuo, and Prof. Yong Liu. My doctoral dissertation consists of RA-L ×2, ICRA ×1, and IJRR ×1 first-authored papers on LiDAR-Visual-Inertial SLAM and 3D Gaussian Splatting, with all code open-sourced (1.5k stars and 160 forks). Beyond this geometry-based 3D vision research track, I have also actively explored video generation and world(-action) models through internships at Amap, Baidu, and GigaAI.
